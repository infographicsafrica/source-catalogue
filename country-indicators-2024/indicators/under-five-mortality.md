# Under-five mortality in 2024

The estimated probability per 1,000 live births of dying before age five under the age-specific mortality rates of the year. [World Bank indicator definition](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.DYN.MORT).

## Coverage

All 54 countries in the UN African States group have a reported value. This edition uses the World Bank data vintage of 8 October 2026.

## Examples from three countries

The values use the unit **Deaths per 1,000 live births** and retain the precision in the archived file.

| Country | Published value |
| --- | ---: |
| Ghana | 35.9 |
| Kenya | 38.8 |
| Senegal | 36.5 |

## Using the data

Compare rates in the same unit. Estimating a number of deaths requires more data than this probability series provides.

Filter `indicator_code` to `SH.DYN.MORT` in the [combined CSV](../AfricaCountryIndicators2024.csv). The `value_status` field identifies which rows have a value. The unit applies to every row for this indicator.
