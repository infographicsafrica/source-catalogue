# People using the internet in 2024

The share of people who used the internet from any location in the previous three months. The definition covers use through phones, computers and other devices. [World Bank indicator definition](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IT.NET.USER.ZS).

## Coverage

The extract has 51 reported values across 54 countries in the UN African States group. Values are blank for Eritrea, South Sudan and Sudan. This edition uses the World Bank data vintage of 8 October 2026.

## Examples from three countries

The values use the unit **% of population** and retain the precision in the archived file.

| Senegal | 60.11780167 |

## Using the data

Use this series for people who used the internet. Subscription counts answer a separate question about connections or accounts.

Filter `indicator_code` to `IT.NET.USER.ZS` in the [combined CSV](../AfricaCountryIndicators2024.csv). The `value_status` field identifies which rows have a value. The unit applies to every row for this indicator.
