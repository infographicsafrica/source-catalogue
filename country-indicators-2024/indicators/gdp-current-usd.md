# GDP in current US dollars in 2024

GDP measures production over the accounting period. This series expresses it in US dollars at current prices. [World Bank indicator definition](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CD).

## Coverage

The extract has 52 reported values across 54 countries in the UN African States group. Values are blank for Eritrea and South Sudan. This edition uses the World Bank data vintage of 8 October 2026.

## Using the data

For growth over time, use a constant-price series. Current-dollar comparisons can reflect price and exchange-rate changes.

Filter `indicator_code` to `NY.GDP.MKTP.CD` in the [combined CSV](../AfricaCountryIndicators2024.csv). The `value_status` field identifies which rows have a value. The unit applies to every row for this indicator.
