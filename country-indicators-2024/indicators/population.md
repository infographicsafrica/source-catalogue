# Total population in 2024

Midyear population estimates count residents regardless of legal status or citizenship. [World Bank indicator definition](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL).

## Coverage

All 54 countries in the UN African States group have a reported value. This edition uses the World Bank data vintage of 8 October 2026.

## Examples from three countries

The values use the unit **People** and retain the precision in the archived file.

| Country | Published value |
| --- | ---: |
| Ghana | 34427414 |
| Kenya | 56432944 |
| Senegal | 18501984 |

## Using the data

Use a matching population year when computing a per-person figure. The year in this snapshot is the estimate’s reference year.

Filter `indicator_code` to `SP.POP.TOTL` in the [combined CSV](../AfricaCountryIndicators2024.csv). The `value_status` field identifies which rows have a value. The unit applies to every row for this indicator.
