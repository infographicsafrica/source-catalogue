# Access to electricity in 2024

The share of people with access to electricity. The World Bank draws on national surveys, industry and international sources. [World Bank indicator definition](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS).

## Coverage

All 54 countries in the UN African States group have a reported value. This edition uses the World Bank data vintage of 8 October 2026.

## Examples from three countries

The values use the unit **% of population** and retain the precision in the archived file.

| Country | Published value |
| --- | ---: |
| Ghana | 91.9 |
| Kenya | 77 |
| Senegal | 82.9 |

## Using the data

Use the percentage for population coverage. Questions about reliability or hours of supply require a measure of service quality.

Filter `indicator_code` to `EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS` in the [combined CSV](../AfricaCountryIndicators2024.csv). The `value_status` field identifies which rows have a value. The unit applies to every row for this indicator.
