# Urban population share in 2024

The share of the population living in areas classed as urban by national statistical offices. The UN Population Division collects and smooths the data. [World Bank indicator definition](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.URB.TOTL.IN.ZS).

## Coverage

All 54 countries in the UN African States group have a reported value. This edition uses the World Bank data vintage of 8 October 2026.

## Examples from three countries

The values use the unit **% of population** and retain the precision in the archived file.

| Country | Published value |
| --- | ---: |
| Ghana | 58.3654868038581 |
| Kenya | 31.8937831721599 |
| Senegal | 55.2368169057304 |

## Using the data

Check the national definitions before comparing small differences across countries. The boundaries and criteria for urban areas can differ.

Filter `indicator_code` to `SP.URB.TOTL.IN.ZS` in the [combined CSV](../AfricaCountryIndicators2024.csv). The `value_status` field identifies which rows have a value. The unit applies to every row for this indicator.
