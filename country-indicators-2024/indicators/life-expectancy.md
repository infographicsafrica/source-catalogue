# Life expectancy at birth in 2024

The years a newborn would live if the period’s mortality patterns stayed the same throughout that person’s life. [World Bank indicator definition](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.IN).

## Coverage

All 54 countries in the UN African States group have a reported value. This edition uses the World Bank data vintage of 8 October 2026.

## Examples from three countries

The values use the unit **Years** and retain the precision in the archived file.

| Country | Published value |
| --- | ---: |
| Ghana | 65.695 |
| Kenya | 63.834 |
| Senegal | 68.924 |

## Using the data

Treat the number as a summary of the year’s mortality pattern. A birth cohort’s eventual average age at death requires following that cohort over time.

Filter `indicator_code` to `SP.DYN.LE00.IN` in the [combined CSV](../AfricaCountryIndicators2024.csv). The `value_status` field identifies which rows have a value. The unit applies to every row for this indicator.
