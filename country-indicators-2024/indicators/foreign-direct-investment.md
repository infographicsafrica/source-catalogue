# Net foreign direct investment inflows in 2024

New direct investment inflows less disinvestment, expressed as a percentage of GDP. The series includes equity, reinvested earnings and other capital recorded in the balance of payments. [World Bank indicator definition](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.KLT.DINV.WD.GD.ZS).

## Coverage

The extract has 52 reported values across 54 countries in the UN African States group. Values are blank for Eritrea and South Sudan. This edition uses the World Bank data vintage of 8 October 2026.

## Using the data

Keep the minus sign when a country has negative net inflows. The net figure includes disinvestment and can be below zero.

Filter `indicator_code` to `BX.KLT.DINV.WD.GD.ZS` in the [combined CSV](../AfricaCountryIndicators2024.csv). The `value_status` field identifies which rows have a value. The unit applies to every row for this indicator.
