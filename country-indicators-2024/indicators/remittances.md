# Personal remittances received in 2024

The World Bank combines transfers between residents and nonresidents with compensation earned by certain short-term and cross-border workers. It expresses the total as a share of the receiving economy’s GDP. [World Bank indicator definition](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.TRF.PWKR.DT.GD.ZS).

## Coverage

The extract has 41 reported values across 54 countries in the UN African States group. Values are blank for Benin, Central African Republic, Chad, Comoros, Equatorial Guinea, Eritrea, Gabon, Libya, Republic of the Congo, Senegal, Somalia, South Sudan and Togo. This edition uses the World Bank data vintage of 8 October 2026.

## Using the data

A larger share can reflect a smaller economy. Use the separate dollar-value series to compare amounts received.

Filter `indicator_code` to `BX.TRF.PWKR.DT.GD.ZS` in the [combined CSV](../AfricaCountryIndicators2024.csv). The `value_status` field identifies which rows have a value. The unit applies to every row for this indicator.
